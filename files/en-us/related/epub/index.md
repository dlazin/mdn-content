EPUB (Electronic Publication) is the W3C open standard file format for digital books and publications. This documentation covers EPUB 3, focusing on reflowable ebooks.

## Guides and concepts

- [Introduction to EPUB](/en-US/docs/Related/EPUB/Overview)
  - : What EPUB is, how reflowable and fixed-layout books differ, where EPUB is supported across reading apps and retailers, and how EPUBs are created.
- [EPUB's relationship to the web](/en-US/docs/Related/EPUB/Relationship_to_the_web)
  - : How EPUB builds on HTML, CSS, SVG, and other Open Web Platform standards.
- [How EPUB differs from the web](/en-US/docs/Related/EPUB/Differences_from_the_web)
  - : How strict XHTML syntax, offline packaging, book metadata, spine reading order, and pagination differ from websites.
